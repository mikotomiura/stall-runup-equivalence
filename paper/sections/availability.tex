\section*{Data and code availability}
\label{sec:availability}

No new data were collected; the analyses used de-identified public datasets under the terms set by their authors. The
preregistration, the deviation, the start records and the results are registered on the Open Science Framework, and the
archive (version 0.2.1; all versions: \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24014042}) holds the estimator and the replay tool at the version used, the code of the
conversion, the evaluation and the explorations, the conditions committed before each computation (in Japanese, with an
English summary) and the aggregate results; Appendix~\ref{app:prereg} gives what it covers and what it does not. The CS1
files are released by their authors on request, and we agreed to publish only aggregate values, so neither the data nor
the per-pause estimate rows are redistributed.
