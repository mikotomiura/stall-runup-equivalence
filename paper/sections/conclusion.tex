\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In the CS1 holdout (139 students), the discrimination AUC of the 30-second change at $u$ of the estimator's two signals,
against the run-up to short pauses by the same writer, fell within the band $[0.45, 0.55]$ set in advance (with a
false-equivalence rate of at most 5.2\% in the calibration, not an upper bound over the whole null), and the registered
threshold table showed no separation (10.3\% and 9.3\% of stall run-ups above each writer's threshold, against 9.6\% of
controls). Outside the registration, the estimator responded to large injected changes but barely to changes of the
observed size, and on the same pairs learners on simple quantities of preceding typing exceeded its 30-second changes.
On this input, the 30-second change of this estimator gave no basis for a trigger before stalls in character production.
The comparison that judged it, against the run-up to short pauses by the same writer with a band set in advance, is
released with its code; whether that choice of partner changes conclusions was not shown.
