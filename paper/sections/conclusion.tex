\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In the CS1 holdout (139 students), the discrimination AUC of the 30-second change at $u$ of the estimator's two signals,
against the run-up to short pauses by the same writer, fell within the band $[0.45, 0.55]$ set in advance, and the
registered threshold table showed no separation (10.3\% and 9.3\% of stall run-ups above each writer's threshold,
against 9.6\% of controls). Outside the registration, the estimator responded to large injected changes but barely to
changes of the observed size, and simple quantities of preceding typing ranked the same pairs higher. On this input, the
30-second change of this estimator gave no basis for a trigger before stalls in character production; comparing a
run-up signal with the run-up to short pauses, with a band set in advance, is how we would check another.
